HERO / Connect – Policy Document Showing Incorrect Premium for Extended Period

Escalation Criteria
Definition
Title
HERO/Connect – Policy Document Showing Incorrect Premium for Extended Bound Period
Summary/Description
Broker bound a policy online for a extended period (08 Feb 2026 – 01 Apr 2027) even though the underwriter originally set the quote for 12 months

HERO UI shows the 12‑month premium and dates.
policy document displayed the correct 14+ month period,
but incorrectly showed the 12‑month premium instead of the prorated 14‑month premium.
Workaround
Reversed the original transactions,
Manually updated the premium values for the 14‑month duration,
Regenerated policy documents.
System information/Environment
HERO /  Connect 
Replication steps
Quote for 12 months.
Bind Policy on Connect for 14+ months .
HERO displays the original 12‑month premium.
When the policy document is generated:
Term duration updates correctly
Premium does not update to prorated 14‑month premium
HERO displays the original 12‑month premium on UI
We attempted to replicate the premium error on the policy document, but were unable to reproduce it. However, the broker was able to trigger the issue on their end.
Expected vs Actual result
Expected
HERO UI and Policy documents should show the correct prorated premium for the 14‑month term.
Actual
Policy documents showed the 14+ month period, But showed the 12‑month premium, creating a mismatch.
HERO UI shows 12 month period and 12 month premium
Affected user(s)
Currently: One broker / single policy
Potentially: Any broker adjusting bound periods
Scope: Unknown if systemic or isolated
